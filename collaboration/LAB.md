# Research → Working Agent: delivery contract
Version 0.1. Proposed learning format; no delivered workshop or research result is claimed.

## Outcome
A participant leaves with a source-linked, replayable artifact, one bounded evaluation and an honest account of what failed. A beautiful demo is useful only when another person can understand its limits and reproduce its intended behaviour.

## Host and producer
The host confirms audience, accessibility, room or call setup, teaching context, consent and institutional supervision. A named producer supports arrival, prerequisites, task timing and help requests. A friend can fill this role when the arrangement and compensation or voluntary basis are explicit.

Use a maximum of 25 learners for the first pilot. Reduce capacity when one facilitator cannot support the actual prerequisite level. A standard edition has a bounded preparation and follow-up allowance; document changes before quoting.

## Before inviting students
1. Select one paper or existing open issue, not a portfolio-wide vision.
2. Inspect full source and any code/data/model licences. Preserve author attribution.
3. Choose a sample task set and a no-paid-API or precomputed route.
4. Run the starter from clean setup; record actual time, failures and environment.
5. Freeze baseline and candidate, metrics, expected cost and a hard execution ceiling.
6. Prepare a fallback recording or local artifact.
7. Confirm student work ownership, contribution options and accessibility.
8. Rehearse with someone who did not write the starter.

Do not advertise a course, scientific reproduction or independent verification before that work exists.

## Ninety-minute experience
| Time | Activity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Show the research question and a real failure | Source and bounded question |
| 10–25 | Separate claim, mechanism and our hypothesis | Claim map |
| 25–50 | Build from a tested starter | Runnable artifact |
| 50–65 | Run the baseline and candidate | Same tasks, metrics and costs |
| 65–80 | Swap reviewer roles and investigate a failure | Review note |
| 80–90 | Demo, export and choose next step | Replay guide and learner reflection |

Technical editions can study retrieval, memory or agent authority. Creative editions can use worldbuilding or research communication, with consistency, provenance and participant agency as evaluation dimensions. Entrepreneurship editions use demand evidence and unit costs; they make no income promises.

## Optional contribution tracks
- Learning only: keep the artifact privately and receive equal teaching support.
- Open contribution: choose an upstream issue or public artifact with clear licence and credit.
- Supervised academic project: agree scope, supervisor, learning outcomes, data and IP terms through the institution.
- Paid commercial mission: start only with a funded brief and accepted compensation; no unpaid trial production.

Assessment must not depend on promoting Starlight, joining a mailing list or assigning exclusive rights. Consent for photos, testimonials or public portfolios is separate.

## Experiment receipt
Use [translation.schema.json](translation.schema.json). Record:
- primary source identifier and inspection scope;
- baseline, candidate, pinned task-set revision and metrics;
- model/harness/tool versions, seeds where relevant and environment;
- actual cost, success and failure cases;
- licence constraints, limitations and independent review;
- upstream submission and acceptance receipts as distinct events.

The schema is a structural contract. Inspect evidence and review conflicts separately. An agent finding a plausible mechanism has produced a hypothesis, not a discovery.

## Acceptance
Proposed pilot target: at least 70% of attendees export an artifact another participant can replay. Report numerator and denominator, incomplete setups and assistance required. Review whether the target was useful after the pilot. Do not manufacture a pass to hit the target.

A research contribution is complete only against its explicit acceptance criteria. A negative result is useful when the protocol and evidence are sound.

## Useful upstream value
Prefer a fixed bug, dependency repair, reproducible installation, evaluation adapter, ablation, multilingual test, accessibility improvement or well-bounded negative result. Ask maintainers through their established contribution route when a change would add maintenance burden. No automated author messaging is authorised by this document.
